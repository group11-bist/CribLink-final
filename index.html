Safety tips before you pay

Simple checks every student should do.

  • Visit the room in person, in daylight and again at night.
  • Ask for the total cost per semester, including every fee.
  • Check the water supply, the lock on your door and the gate.
  • Never send money before you have seen the room and the landlord.
  • Take a friend, and tell someone where you are going.
